# Videogo Boundary

Videogo is an independent commercial backend and technology partner led by Jacopo. It retains video and content-production technology IP, including software and generic production methods for video, carousel, social and advertising outputs.

Videogo receives only task-scoped approved inputs and returns outputs through the task gateway. It receives no vault access.
